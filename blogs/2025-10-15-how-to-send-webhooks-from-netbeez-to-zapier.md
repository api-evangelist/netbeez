Do you want to automate workflows with data from your NetBeez dashboard? You can easily send notifications of alerts or incidents from NetBeez to Zapier using webhooks . Here’s how: In Zapier , create a new Zap and choose “Webhooks by Zapier” → “Catch Raw Hook” as the trigger.
