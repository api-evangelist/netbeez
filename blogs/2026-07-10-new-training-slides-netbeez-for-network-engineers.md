We published a new NetBeez training deck designed for network engineers who want a practical overview of how to use NetBeez for continuous network monitoring and troubleshooting. This training introduces the main NetBeez building blocks: agents, targets, scheduled tests, Wi-Fi networks, alert profiles, incidents, notifications, and integrations. The slides explain how they work together to provide end-to-end visibility from the user, branch, data center, cloud, or remote endpoint perspective.
