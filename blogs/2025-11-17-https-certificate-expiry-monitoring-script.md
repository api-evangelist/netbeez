Title: HTTPS Certificate Expiry Monitoring Script 1. Introduction TLS certificates are a fundamental security layer for any HTTPS-enabled service. When a certificate expires—or worse, is revoked—clients will fail to connect, user-facing applications will break, and integrations may stop working unexpectedly.
