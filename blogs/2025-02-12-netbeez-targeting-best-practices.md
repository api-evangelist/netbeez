Introduction As your Network evolves and needs change, one of the biggest questions we’ve been asked is, what should we monitor? This overview should provide some insight, explain the thought process involved and provide some high level examples of targets and testing. Targets vs Tests (Terminology) When we refer to a target, we refer to every test and resource configured under it.
