1. Introduction When diagnosing Wi‑Fi congestion or planning coverage, a quick snapshot of nearby networks by band (2.4/5/6 GHz) is invaluable. This script scans the air and reports visible BSSIDs total , a breakdown per 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz / 6 GHz , and the count of unique channels observed .
