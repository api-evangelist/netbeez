1. Introduction For network engineers and administrators, knowing how many Wi-Fi networks are nearby can be useful for interference analysis, troubleshooting, or coverage planning. This Bash script scans the Wi-Fi interface, counts the number of unique SSIDs, and reports the result in a NetBeez-friendly format.
